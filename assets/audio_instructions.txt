AUDIO FILES NEEDED
==================
Place the following .mp3 files in this directory:

1. intro_music.mp3    - Short intro/jingle music for splash screen (3-5 seconds)
2. background_music.mp3 - Looping background music for gameplay
3. success.mp3        - Short positive sound for correct answers
4. error.mp3          - Short negative sound for wrong answers
5. applause.mp3       - Applause sound for winning the puzzle
6. sad_tone.mp3       - Sad/melancholy sound for losing
7. cheer.mp3          - Cheering sound for winning
8. boo.mp3            - Booing sound for losing

HOW TO GET FREE AUDIO FILES:
-----------------------------
You can download free sound effects from:
- Pixabay Sound Effects: https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/
- Freesound: https://freesound.org/
- Zapsplat: https://www.zapsplat.com/

Search for appropriate sounds and download them as .mp3 files.
Rename them to match the filenames above.
